A few useful answers

Do I need to paste my Instagram token?

For this hosted connection, no. Sign-in saves it on the Pi in encrypted storage. The browser receives a private session, not your Instagram access token.

Can I manage more than one account?

Yes. Sign in with the account you want to manage. Each verified account has its own workspace and pause controls.

Can I run it myself?

Yes. The public source uses SQLite by default and Neon is optional. Self-hosting requires your own Meta app and your own configured Pi/connector.

Will it send messages immediately after sign-in?

New workspaces start paused. Review your flow settings and activate the account and flows you intend to use.
