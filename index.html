INSTAGRAM CONNECTOR

Connect your Instagram account

Configure this deployment with your own Meta app ID, app secret and redirect URL before authorizing Instagram. Sign in with an Instagram Business or Creator account you control. Approve access to your profile, comments, and messages, then copy the account’s access token into your own Pi installation.

Continue with Instagram

Choose the account you want to connect on Instagram’s sign-in page. Meta’s app approval and tester access requirements apply. Personal accounts need to switch to Business or Creator to use this connection.

Your token and Instagram account ID appear after approval. Keep the token private and save it in your bot’s private configuration. This Vercel page does not retain the token. Self-hosting independently requires your own Meta app and connector; this token alone does not set up your Pi’s signed webhook receiver.
